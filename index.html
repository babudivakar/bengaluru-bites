Our Story

Homemade with Heart

Bengaluru Bites is a home-based catering and baking venture in Galway, born out of a passion for authentic home cooking. We bring the warmth of a home kitchen and the richness of traditional flavours right here to the West of Ireland.

Whether it's a birthday cake, a party spread, a festive feast, or a box of crispy snacks — we make everything fresh to order with the finest ingredients and a whole lot of love. From intimate gatherings to large celebrations, we cater across Galway and surrounding areas.
